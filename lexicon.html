The lexicon

Coinages, in the order they were made. yours marks the words you brought; mine marks suggestions of mine that survived.

disclaimerciouslyadverbyours
dis·clai·MER·cious·ly

Publishing something extravagant while affixing a disclaimer that absolves nothing and advertises everything. Of an object: bearing a disclaimer as composition rather than appendix — the hedge given margin, weight and iconography.

He posted this image disclaimerciously.

Note. The buried mercy was never designed in. A disclaimer is a plea for clemency entered in advance, and the word admits it under its breath.

followancenounyours
FOL·low·ance

Subscription sustained without attention; the dutiful maintenance of a following one does not read.

Her feed was mostly followance.

Note. Licensed outright by allowance — the same -ow+-ance junction. On the model of attendance (presence without engagement) and observance (practice without belief).

followificationnounyours

The conversion of every relation — colleagues, clergy, physicians, friends — into an account one subscribes to asymmetrically.

The followification of the newsroom.

Relation. The process; followance is what it leaves behind.

loopholificationnounyours

The restructuring of a rule system around its exceptions, continued until the exceptions constitute the system.

Procurement reform ended in quiet loopholification.

intertwinificationnounyours

Entanglement pursued as policy — things bound together so that they cannot later be separated. Vendor lock-in, forced bundling, the merger structured to be unwindable.

The contract was less a partnership than an intertwinification.

reprogramificationnounyours

The doctrine that any settled thing — a habit, a belief, an institution, a culture — is a codebase awaiting a patch.

Every self-help shelf in the airport preaches reprogramification.

preventificationnounyours

The condition of an institution in which every process exists to prevent rather than to do; the risk register eating the whole job.

preventfullyadverbyours

Out of a temperament organised around what one is avoiding — as distinct from preventively, which describes a purpose rather than a disposition.

He lived preventfully, and had nothing to show for it but the absence of disasters.

successificationnounyours

Work restructured around the measurement of winning, until the measuring is the work.

clusterfuckificationnounyours

The mechanism, as opposed to the state — the incremental path by which individually reasonable decisions arrive at disaster with nobody having chosen it.

Six months of clusterfuckification, and not one meeting where anyone decided anything wrong.

compostificationnounyours

Generative decay; breakdown that is the point. The dead thing becoming the material of the next thing.

Two years of compostification, and the failed company had fed four better ones.

Note. The only honest agent-deletion in the collection. Nothing decomposes a compost pile — bacteria, heat, time. Elsewhere the suffix hides a hand that is really there; here there is genuinely no hand.

warificationnounyours

The reframing of a non-war thing as war — a trial, a policy dispute, a newsroom argument.

Warification deployed by a defendant against the court trying him.

couldismnounyours

Possibility held as identity. Faces forward — the novel that is always going to be written, the potential one is perpetually sitting on.

wouldismnounyours

The counterfactual held as identity. Faces back — I would have been a—

Note. Could and would are past tenses doing non-past work, because English marks unreality as pastness. The bases carry "not real" before the suffix adds anything. The third member is taken: Karen Horney named the tyranny of the should in 1950.

wordsmithinglyadverbyours

Polishing the wording while the substance sits untouched.

They spent the meeting wordsmithingly, and the problem was exactly where they left it.

vibepromptinglyadverbyours

Building by asks given as moods rather than specifications. The input end of the thing vibe coding names at the output end.

He created new software vibepromptingly, then read every line of it.

vibeliciousadjectiveyours

Of an atmosphere: consumed with appetite rather than merely present. Vibey describes a room; vibelicious describes wanting to eat it.

warcriminaliciousadjectiveyours

Relishing the spectacle of an accused inverting the frame on the tribunal. Lives only in lurid register — in neutral prose the suffix means the wrong thing.

absurdalisticadjectiveyours

The absurd performed as a genre rather than arrived at. The phantom syllable enacts the pomposity it names.

higherdimensionalshapeshifterouslyadverbyours

In the manner of meaning asserted by accumulation — on the theory that enough stacked components must add up to profundity. Self-demonstrating, as sesquipedalian is a long word for long words.

rebracketinglyadverbyours

Listening in such a way that the seams fall somewhere other than where the history put them.

She heard the word rebracketingly, and a new suffix fell out.

unhappeningnounmine

The removal of an occurred event from the record; an incident officially declared not to have taken place while the people who lived it remain.

Pair. The unhappened — harm that never occurred because someone prevented it, the achievement that leaves no evidence. -ed never was; -ing was and then was not.

infographicitynounmine

Evidentiary appearance in excess of evidentiary content. The bar chart without an axis; the arrow diagram asserting a causation nobody measured.

Provenance. Proposed by you as infographicously. The suffix was blocked on rule, but the replacement changed suffix, part of speech and meaning — the last supplied rather than asked for. The only entry here not descended from the word that prompted it.

ponderatiouslyadverbmine

Performing thought without conducting any — the held pause, the stroked chin, the hmm that precedes no consideration.

Note. Probably unnecessary, and doubly shaky. Ponderingly was legal all along, off the original form, and was ruled out on an overstated rule. Worse, -atious is licensed by an -ation noun underneath — ostentation, flirtation, vexation, disputation — and ponderation is archaic. Kept as a record of the reasoning, not as a recommendation.

successitiouslyadverbmine

In a manner that performs success; the win assembled after the fact, the metric chosen once the number was known.

Note. An ear-word, not a rule-word — -itious is inherited and no longer productive. It works by echo of fictitious, which is also the nearest neighbour by sense.

Words that turned out to be real

Proposed here, then found already living in the language. Half the work of coining is discovering you didn't need to.

floordrobe
The clothing stratum on the bedroom floor, used as storage. Already in dictionaries. Wardrobe is Old French warderobe, "guard-robe" — so there is no morpheme drobe, and the blend is the seventh false seam in this project.
planification
Institutional planning at ministry scale, borrowed from French. Planning has a planner in it; planification has no doer-slot at all, which is the feature.
complexification
The process of growing more complex, as distinct from complication, which names a discrete snag.
chutzpah
Its canonical illustration — the man who murders his parents and pleads for mercy as an orphan — is exactly the inversion two entries here went looking for.
victoriously · infographically · unhingedly · ponderingly
All regular, all understood on first contact, all reached for by coinage before anyone checked whether the rules had already built them.

How the words were made

One suffix, one move.Each suffix names a destination. They are alternate roads, not successive rungs — -ification plus -ously gains syllables without gaining density.
-ification wants a noun.Loophole, success, compost worked. Intertwine, reprogram, unhappen, prevent, expand are round trips, because -ify's only job is making a verb out of something that isn't one.
Compounds buy syllables.Secondary stress keeps a long tail from collapsing. WORD-smith-ing-ly survives four syllables; VIC-to-ri-ful cannot.
Check whether the rules already built it.Say it aloud. If it sounds merely unfamiliar rather than invented, it is not a coinage — it is English you hadn't happened to use.
The suffix must carry the meaning.Not just the part of speech. -licious encodes appetite, -ification encodes process. Neither encodes inversion — that is prefix territory.
Inspect the typos.Three accidents paid: mercy inside the founding word, Middle English -tioun from a slipped u, and a three-hundred-year-old split between flour and flower.
Seams are how suffixes are born.Hearing a chunk where the join isn't gave English -gate (a hotel), -athon (a place), -holic, -tastic, -splain, -burger, -licious and -drobe. The only difference between the error and the innovation is uptake.
Forty-six proposals, seven false seams, three paid accidents, five corrections to the rules — every one of those caught by ear rather than by argument. The working table, with everything rejected and why, is in index.html.
