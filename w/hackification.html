hackification

noun   coined here

hack·i·fi·CA·tion

The conversion of ordinary objects into attack surface — fridges, doorbells, pacemakers, thermostats.

Hackification could have occurred.

Etymologyhack + -ification. Noun base, clean chain, no squatter: hacking has a hacker in it, this has none.

NoteDeployed with 'could have occurred', which is breach-notification English — no actor, no certainty. Both halves delete the responsible party: the noun removes the hacker, the modal removes the event.
