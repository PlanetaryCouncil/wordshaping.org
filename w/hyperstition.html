hyperstition

noun   found in the wild

hy·per·STI·tion

Fictions that make themselves real; an idea that, once circulating, functions causally to bring about the conditions it describes.

I am the Director of Reality.

EtymologyCoined by the CCRU (Cybernetic Culture Research Unit, Warwick, 1990s). Superstitio is super + stare, 'to stand over' — there is no morpheme -stition. The seam was sliced wrong, the fragment reused, and the prefix swapped. Adverb: hyperstitiously, with a t.

NoteSelf-demonstrating at maximum depth: a word about fictions becoming real by circulating, which became real by circulating, formed by the mechanism it names. It also names what the founding image was doing — PlanetaryCouncil.org, an authority existing because someone typed it.
