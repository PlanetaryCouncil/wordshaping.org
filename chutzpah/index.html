KHUTS·pah

As first written
chutzpah
Stands today exactly as it was first set down.

Audacity of a kind that inverts the frame — the offender claiming the victim's standing.

Travelling internationally with an active arrest warrant to a city that does not welcome you.

EtymologyYiddish, from Hebrew ḥuṣpāh.

NoteIts canonical illustration — the man who murders his parents then pleads for mercy as an orphan — is exactly the structure two entries here went hunting for. Tu quoque and DARVO are the technical siblings; this is the ordinary-English one.
